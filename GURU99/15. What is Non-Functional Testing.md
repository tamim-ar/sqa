## What is Non-Functional Testing?

* Testing non-functional aspects: **performance, usability, reliability, scalability, etc.**
* Ensures system readiness for non-functional parameters not covered in functional testing.
* Example: testing how many users can log in simultaneously.

---

## Objectives

* Improve **usability, efficiency, maintainability, portability**.
* Reduce production **risks and costs**.
* Optimize **installation, setup, execution, monitoring**.
* Collect **metrics and measurements**.
* Enhance knowledge of system behavior and technologies.

---

## Characteristics

* Must be **measurable**, not subjective.
* Exact values may be unknown early.
* Requirements must be **prioritized**.
* Identify **quality attributes** correctly.

---

## Key Parameters

1. **Security** – protection from attacks. (Security Testing)
2. **Reliability** – consistent functioning. (Reliability Testing)
3. **Survivability** – recovery from failures. (Recovery Testing)
4. **Availability** – system uptime. (Stability Testing)
5. **Usability** – ease of use. (Usability Testing)
6. **Scalability** – ability to handle growth. (Scalability Testing)
7. **Interoperability** – working with other systems. (Interoperability Testing)
8. **Efficiency** – response time, capacity handling.
9. **Flexibility** – adaptability to configurations.
10. **Portability** – transfer across environments.
11. **Reusability** – reuse in other applications.
